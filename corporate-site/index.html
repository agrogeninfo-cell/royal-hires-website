EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Executive leadership

Udaya Kumara.

People-Centred Leadership. Strategic Business Growth. Workforce Excellence.

Udaya Kumara is the Executive Director of Royal Hires Private Limited, bringing over five years of professional experience in business management, with a strong focus on people management, manufacturing and workforce solutions.

As part of the company’s executive leadership, he contributes to shaping its strategic direction, strengthening operational performance and developing productive relationships with corporate clients, employees and business partners. His professional background supports a practical understanding of organisational management and the workforce requirements of the manufacturing sector.

With a people-centred leadership approach, he is committed to building dependable workforce solutions that support business growth while creating meaningful employment opportunities.

Professional Experience

5+ years of experience across business management and organisational development, people management and workforce coordination, manufacturing-sector workforce requirements, employee engagement and productivity, business relationship development, operational planning, service quality, organisational efficiency and sustainable growth.

Areas of Leadership

  • People Management — Employee engagement, workforce coordination and a positive working environment.
  • Business Management — Organisational planning, operational discipline and business development.
  • Manufacturing & Workforce Solutions — Factory workforce requirements and practical manpower solutions.
  • Strategic Development — Sustainable growth through clear priorities and continuous improvement.
  • Corporate Relationships — Professional, reliable relationships with clients and business partners.

Leadership Philosophy

“Behind every successful organisation is a strong team, a shared vision, and leadership that understands the value of people.”

Vision & Strategic Direction

Building a Leading Workforce Solutions Company in Sri Lanka.

  • Expanding presence across manufacturing and industrial sectors
  • Developing long-term partnerships with corporate clients

  • Creating meaningful and sustainable employment opportunities

Commitment to Excellence
