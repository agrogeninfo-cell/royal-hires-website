Payroll calculation rules

  • Full day = 1.0 · Half day = 0.5 · Absent = 0
  • Late deduction = configured hourly rate ÷ 60 × late minutes
  • Attendance bonus applies at or above configured qualifying days
  • Meal deduction = recorded breakfast + lunch + dinner counts × configured rates
  • Factory invoice excludes worker late deductions and meal deductions
  • Advance defaults to Rs. 0 and can be entered in the payroll review before saving
