Who we are

A stronger workforce starts with the right people.

Royal Hires Private Limited is a Sri Lankan manpower solutions agency focused on helping businesses build reliable teams. We bring people and operational support together, with a practical understanding of the demands of factory environments.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the need, connect the right people, and support the work that keeps businesses moving.
