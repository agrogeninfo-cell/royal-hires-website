Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Need more information? Contact our team for details specific to your staffing or employment inquiry.

What services does Royal Hires provide?

We provide recruitment and staff placement, temporary and contract staffing, permanent recruitment, manpower supply, executive recruitment, HR consultancy, and payroll and HR support.

Which areas do you serve?

Our stated service area is Sri Lanka’s Western Province. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

How can a job seeker contact Royal Hires?

Job seekers can contact us by WhatsApp or email at royalhiresinfo@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive personal information until an appropriate secure submission method is provided.

How can a business request staff?

Use the client inquiry form or contact us by phone or WhatsApp to describe your workforce requirements.
